# References

Numbered citations used in `docs/gym_env.md`, `README.md`, and `experiments/rl/README.md`.

- [1] Sutton, R. S. and Barto, A. G. *Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction*. 2nd ed. MIT Press, 2018. MDP formalism (S, A, P, R, gamma); episodic return; policy-gradient setup assumed throughout.
- [2] Towers, M. et al. Gymnasium: A Standard Interface for Reinforcement Learning Environments. arXiv:2407.17032, 2024. Successor to OpenAI Gym; defines `reset(seed, options)`, `step` returning `(obs, reward, terminated, truncated, info)`, `check_env`, and environment registration. This repo conforms to that API.
- [3] Williams, R. J. Simple Statistical Gradient-Following Algorithms for Connectionist Reinforcement Learning. *Machine Learning* 8:229-256, 1992. REINFORCE with mean-return baseline; implemented in `finrl/rl/policy.py` as a linear-softmax baseline only.
- [4] Schulman, J. et al. Proximal Policy Optimization Algorithms. arXiv:1707.06347, 2017. Clipped surrogate objective; used here via Stable-Baselines3 with `MultiInputPolicy` for Dict observations and MultiDiscrete actions.
- [5] Raffin, A. et al. Stable-Baselines3: Reliable Reinforcement Learning Implementations. *JMLR* 22(268):1-8, 2021. PPO implementation, `Monitor`/`EvalCallback`/`DummyVecEnv` used in `finrl/rl/train_sb3.py`.
- [6] Ng, A. Y., Harada, D., and Russell, S. Policy Invariance Under Reward Transformations: Theory and Application to Reward Shaping. ICML, 1999. Potential-based shaping `F(s,a,s') = gamma*Phi(s') - Phi(s)` preserves the optimal policy; arbitrary shaping bonuses do not carry that guarantee. The intermediate bonuses in §3 are engineering shaping in this sense and are reported as such (see Limitations).
- [7] Shao, Z. et al. DeepSeekMath: Pushing the Limits of Mathematical Reasoning in Open Language Models. arXiv:2402.03300, 2024. Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO): PPO variant that replaces the learned critic with group-relative baselines. `finrl/rl/train_grpo_stub.py` follows that reward interface with `dense_report_reward` as the outcome reward.
- [8] Lambert, N. et al. Tulu 3: Pushing Frontiers in Open Language Model Post-Training. arXiv:2411.15124, 2024. Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards (RLVR): RL against deterministic correctness checks rather than a learned reward model. `checkpoints/rlvr_dataset.jsonl` is structured for that setting (`prompt` / `ground_truth_pipe` kept separate).
- [9] U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Regulation NMS, Rule 605 (17 CFR 242.605; formerly Rule 11Ac1-5, adopted 2000; amendments adopted 2024, Release No. 34-99679) and the Rule 605 NMS Plan (pipe-delimited reporting specification). Defines covered orders, order-type and order-size categorization, execution-quality statistics (effective/quoted/realized spreads, price improvement), and the monthly pipe-delimited disclosure format this repo reproduces in reduced form (24 columns; `finrl/rules/serializer.py`).
