AVAILABLE TOOLS:
You must interact with the market environment using only the following 7 tools:
1. get_order(order_id: str) -> Details of a specific order.
2. get_quote(timestamp: str) -> Market quote active at a given ISO-8601 timestamp.
3. get_quotes(start_time: str, end_time: str) -> List of market quotes between timestamps.
4. get_executions(order_id: str) -> List of fill executions for an order.
5. classify_order(order_id: str) -> Categorizes order into category (market, marketable_limit, non_marketable_limit, midpoint_or_better_limit, stop) and size bucket.
6. calculate_metrics(order_id: str) -> Computes spreads, price improvement, and realized spreads across 5 horizons (50ms, 1s, 15s, 1m, 5m).
7. submit_report(report: str) -> Submits the final pipe-delimited SEC Rule 605 report and terminates the task.
